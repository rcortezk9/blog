Daily Formation Synthesis.

What is the Church teaching your soul today?

Today the Church calls you to get up from the table where you keep count. Levi sat there every day, gathering and keeping, and no one expected him to change. Then Christ looked at him and said two words, and he walked away from it all and set a feast. This is what the resurrection does to a person. If death has lost its sting, then nothing you give in Christ is lost. Your labor is "not in vain." Your coin for a stranger is not wasted. Your open table is not a risk. The Pharisees still stand at the door and ask why Christ eats with people like you. Let them ask. You are not at His table because you are well. You are there because He is the Physician and you came to be healed. And the first sign of that healing is often very plain. The hand that used to close starts to open. So keep the Lord's Day the way Paul asked: worship, then give. Set something aside, a little, every week, in proportion to what God has given. Offer back to Him His own, on behalf of all and for all.

Ascetic posture for today: Before you spend anything on yourself today, set something aside for someone who cannot repay you, and say, "Thine own of Thine own."

From the Orthodox Study Bible

1 Corinthians 15:58The doctrine of the resurrection has a practical consequence: It is to move us to do the works that God would have us do, the work of the Lord.
Luke 5:27Levi (Matthew) answers Christ’s call, “Follow Me,” and leaves his occupation to become a disciple. From the beginning of His ministry Christ has been a friend of tax collectors and sinners, which is one of the Pharisees’ complaints against Him (v. 30). Levi may also have been one of the tax collectors prepared for Christ by John the Baptist (3:12).
Luke 5:29This feast expresses Matthew’s joy and gratitude. The guest register is a stirring demonstration of the fruit of Jesus’ love and forgiveness.

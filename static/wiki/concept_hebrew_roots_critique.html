Summary

The corpus provides systematic refutations of two related errors: the Hebrew Roots Movement (HRM), which requires Christians to observe Mosaic Law (Sabbath, feasts, dietary laws) for righteousness or spiritual growth, and Christian Zionism / "neo-Judaizing," which assigns special theological status to modern ethnic Jews and the state of Israel. Both errors share a common root: equivocation on the meaning of "Jew," "Israel," and "Judaism," combined with a failure to recognize the New Covenant's supersession of the Old. Sources span Reformed Protestant, Traditional Catholic, and Reformed postmillennialist perspectives.

Key Points

  • Core error shared by both movements: Conflating Old Covenant identity/observance with New Covenant life in Christ. Both movements exploit category confusion about the words "Jew," "Israel," and "Judaism."
  • HRM is a works-righteousness system: Adding Torah observance (Sabbath, feasts, dietary laws, circumcision) to faith for justification or sanctification is the Galatian heresy (Gal 1:6-9; 5:4). Paul calls it "another gospel" and says it severs one from Christ.
  • HRM cannot observe what it claims to observe: Legitimate biblical feast observance requires a Temple, functioning Levitical priesthood, and animal sacrifices. None exists. Modern HRM feasts are self-invented approximations, not biblical observance (no calendar, no temple, no sacrifice). James 2:10: "whoever keeps the whole Law and yet stumbles at one point is guilty of all."
  • HRM draws from Talmudic Judaism, not Scripture: Participants study the Babylonian Talmud, adopt Kabbalistic practices, and use Talmudic calendar systems — despite the Talmud being a post-70 AD human composition with no divine authority over Christians.
  • Eschatological danger: HRM advocacy for a Third Temple and renewed Jewish sacrifice system is, theologically, advocacy for overturning Christ's once-for-all sacrifice. The corpus identifies this as preparation for eschatological deception.
  • "Judeo-Christianity" is a post-WWII construct: The term erases the fundamental theological difference between Christianity (Jesus is Messiah; Church is true Israel) and Rabbinic Judaism (Jesus rejected as Messiah; Church is apostate). Accepting the "Judeo-Christian" framework functionally abandons Christian distinctiveness.
  • Neo-Judaizers exploit equivocation: Both HRM and Christian Zionist apologists systematically exploit the ambiguity of "Jew" (4+ meanings), "Judaism" (Second Temple vs. Rabbinic), and "Israel" (7+ meanings) to construct straw man arguments against Christian supersessionism. Once terms are precisely defined, the arguments collapse.
  • Multi-tradition consensus against these errors: Reformed Protestant (Shamoun, Fannin/Powell), Traditional Catholic (Catholic State/Christ The King), and Reformed postmillennialist (Isker/Webbin) all arrive at the same refutation from different angles.

Details

The Hebrew Roots Movement — Who Is It?

HRM is a contemporary movement teaching that Christians must observe the Mosaic Law to properly follow God. Key claims:

  • The name "Jesus" is pagan; must use "Yeshua"
  • Christians must observe Sabbath on Saturday
  • Christians must keep the biblical feasts (Passover, Shavuot, Sukkot, etc.)
  • Dietary laws (kosher) apply to Christians
  • Tzitzit (tassels) and other physical markers should be worn
  • The Church replaced authentic "Hebrew" Christianity

Matt Powell (insider testimony): at 17 he was drawn in by claims to return to "the original apostles" and "true Messiah Yeshua." By 19 he had left, recognizing the movement put him in bondage — including the expectation of killing animals for feasts.

Why HRM Fails Theologically (Reformed Response)

  1. 1 Corinthians 9:20-21: Paul categorically distinguishes being "under the Law" (Mosaic) from being "under the Law of Christ." Christians have undergone a covenantal jurisdiction change.
  2. Hebrews 7:12: "For when there is a change in the priesthood, there is necessarily a change in the law." Christ's Melchizedek priesthood replaced Aaron's; the law establishing Aaron's priesthood is correspondingly superseded.
  3. Colossians 2:16-17: "Let no one pass judgment on you in questions of food and drink, or with regard to a festival or a new moon or a Sabbath. These are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance belongs to Christ."
  4. Galatians 3:10: "All who rely on works of the law are under a curse" — because no one can keep the whole law.
  5. Hebrews 10:14: "By a single offering He has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified." Christ's work is tetelestai — paid in full. No supplementation by feast observance is possible or required.

Neo-Judaizers and Christian Zionism (Catholic / Postmillennialist Response)

"Neo-Judaizers" (Catholic State's term) are Catholics and Protestants who:

  • Deny or soften the Church's supersession of ethnic Israel
  • Assign unconditional theological status to the modern state of Israel
  • Treat support for Israeli politics as a Christian duty
  • Accept a dual-covenant framework (Jews saved through Sinai covenant; Gentiles through Christ)

The "Judeo-Christian" term (Isker/Webbin) is critiqued as a post-WWII construct that:

  • Erases the fundamental NT declaration that Christianity superseded Judaism
  • Functionally prevents Christian truth claims in public discourse
  • Treats the Holocaust as the primary moral reference point in place of the Cross

Matthew 21:33-46 (Parable of the Tenants) is the key text: Jesus declares that the Kingdom of God will be "taken away from you and given to a people producing its fruits" (v. 43). This is Christ's own announcement of the covenant transfer.

Equivocation as the Root Method

The Catholic State identifies seven distinct meanings of "Israel" that are routinely conflated:

  1. The patriarch Jacob (personal name)
  2. The 12 tribes (ethnic/genealogical)
  3. The Northern Kingdom (political)
  4. The Southern Kingdom (political)
  5. The Jewish people of the OT (pre-Christ)
  6. The Church — the New Israel (NT)
  7. The modern political state (post-1948)

Debates about Israel's status become impossible to resolve honestly if participants use different meanings across premises. The solution is definitional clarity before argument.

Personal Testimony Value (Matt Powell)

Matt Powell's insider account is a key apologetic resource because:

  • He describes HRM's seductive appeal (false claims of returning to "authentic" apostolic faith)
  • He documents the bondage: "having to kill animals... if it's a requirement, it is a form of bondage"
  • He describes studying the Babylonian Talmud as a Christian, believing it unlocked biblical truth
  • His deliverance came through recognizing sola fide and Christ's finished work

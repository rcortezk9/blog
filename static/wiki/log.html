  • Notes: Opened to fill the gap flagged in the reading-list ingest. Frames theoria as one faculty in two registers — hermeneutical (Antiochene contemplative reading, the middle path between literalism and allegory, rooted in history) and ascetic-mystical (vision of the uncreated light; katharsis → theoria → theosis). The hermeneutical sense was the true gap: the contemplative sense was already treated in Experiences of Theosis — Theoria, the Uncreated Light, and the Communion of Saints but never named as the same faculty that reads Scripture.

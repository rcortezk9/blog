A Daily Prayer Rule

Morning · Evening · Common Prayers · Holy Communion · The Departed · Family

Morning Prayers

Arise and Begin — enter the presence of God, ready to engage in unseen warfare, and make the Sign of the Cross, saying:

Usual Beginning

In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

Glory to You, our God, glory to You.

O Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of Truth, who are everywhere present and fill all things, Treasury of good gifts and Giver of Life, come and abide in us, and cleanse us of all impurity, and save our souls, O Good One.

Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us. (3 times)

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Most Holy Trinity, have mercy on us. O Lord, wash away our sins, O Master, pardon our transgressions. O Holy One, visit and heal our infirmities, for Your Name’s sake.

Lord, have mercy. (3 times)

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your Name. Your Kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.

Troparia to the Holy Trinity

As we rise from sleep we worship You, O good and powerful Lord, and to You we sing the Angel’s hymn: Holy, Holy, Holy are You, O God. Through the prayers of the Theotokos, have mercy upon us.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.

Having raised me from my bed, and from my sleep, O Lord, enlighten my mind and heart, and open my mouth to praise You, O Holy Trinity, saying: Holy, Holy, Holy are You, O God. Through the prayers of the Theotokos, have mercy upon us.

Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Suddenly the Judge shall come, and the deeds of each of us shall be laid bare. But at midnight, let us cry with fear saying: Holy, Holy, Holy are You, O God. Through the prayers of the Theotokos, have mercy upon us.

Troparia of My Patron Saints

Holy Right-Believing Prince Alexander Nevsky — Tone 4

O blessed Alexander, you were like a revered sprout from a godly root, for Christ revealed you as a sacred treasure of the Russian land, and a new, most glorious and God-pleasing wonderworker. Today, as we gather on your Feast Day with faith and love, rejoicing with Psalms and spiritual hymns, we glorify the Lord who gave you the grace of healing. Entreat Him to save our city and land, that those who dwell in them may be pleasing to God, and that all Orthodox Christians may be saved.

Holy Great Martyr Demetrios of Thessalonica — Tone 3

The entire world has found you to be a great champion in times of peril; for you put the heathen to flight, O victorious one. As you brought to naught the boasts of Lyaeus, and gave courage to Nestor in the stadium, in the same way, O Holy Great Martyr Demetrios, entreat Christ God, that He grant us great mercy.

Thanksgiving Prayer of St. Basil the Great

To the Most Holy Trinity

As I rise from sleep I thank You, O Holy Trinity, because of the abundance of Your goodness and long-suffering You have not been upset with me a sinner, nor let me perish in my iniquities; but have shown Your usual love for mankind, and have raised me up when I had fallen into despair, to hasten to praise Your power. Enlighten the eyes of my mind and open my mouth to meditate on Your words, to understand Your commandments, to do Your will, to sing to You in heartfelt confession, and to praise Your all-holy Name, of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Prayer of the Wise Virgins

Glory to You, Almighty King and God; for in Your love for mankind, You have prepared me, an unworthy sinner, to rise from sleep and to gain entrance into Your holy house. Accept, O Lord, the voice of my supplication, as from Your holy and noetic powers. Be pleased that my song, issuing from impure lips, be offered to You with a pure heart and a spirit of humility, that I too may become a companion of the wise virgins, with the bright lamp of my soul, that I may glorify You, Word of God, glorified in the Father and in the Holy Spirit. Amen.

O come let us worship God our King. O come let us worship and fall down before Christ our King and God. O come let us worship and fall down before Christ Himself, our King and God.

Psalm of Repentance

Psalm 50

Have mercy on me, O God, according to Your great mercy; and according to the multitude of Your compassions, blot out my transgression.

Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

For I am conscious of my iniquity; and my sin is continually before me.

Against You only have I sinned, and done evil before You, that You might be justified in Your words, and might prevail when You are judged.

For behold, I was conceived in iniquities, and in sin my mother conceived me.

For behold, You love truth; You have revealed to me the secret and hidden things of Your wisdom.

You will sprinkle me with hyssop, and I shall be purified. You will wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow.

You will cause me to hear joy and gladness; the bones that are humbled shall rejoice.

Turn away Your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.

Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.

Cast me not away from Your presence, and take not Your Holy Spirit from me.

Restore to me the joy of Your salvation, and establish me with Your governing Spirit.

Then I will teach transgressors Your ways, and the ungodly shall turn back to You.

Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, the God of my salvation, and my tongue shall joyfully declare Your righteousness.

O Lord, You will open my lips, and my mouth shall declare Your praise.

For if You desired sacrifice, I would have given it; You will not take pleasure in whole burnt offerings.

A sacrifice to God is a broken spirit; a broken and humbled heart God will not despise.

Do good, O Lord, to Sion in Your good pleasure; and let the walls of Jerusalem be built.

Then You shall be pleased with a sacrifice of righteousness, the offering and whole burnt sacrifices; then they shall offer calves upon Your altar.

Symbol of Faith

The Creed

I believe in one God, Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and of all things visible and invisible.

And in one Lord Jesus Christ, the only-begotten Son of God, begotten of the Father before all ages. Light of Light, true God of true God, begotten, not created, of one essence with the Father, through Whom all things were made.

Who for us men and for our salvation came down from heaven and was incarnate of the Holy Spirit and the Virgin Mary and became man. He was crucified for us under Pontius Pilate, and suffered and was buried. And He rose on the third day according to the Scriptures. He ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Father. And He will come again with glory to judge the living and the dead. His kingdom shall have no end.

And in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the Giver of Life, Who proceeds from the Father, Who together with the Father and the Son is worshiped and glorified, Who spoke through the prophets.

In one, Holy, Catholic, and Apostolic Church. I confess one baptism for the forgiveness of sins. I look for the resurrection of the dead, and the life of the age to come. Amen.

Morning Prayer of the Optina Elders

by St. Philaret of Moscow

O Lord, grant that I may meet all that this coming day brings to me with spiritual tranquility. Grant that I may fully surrender myself to Your holy Will.

At every hour of this day, direct and support me in all things. Whatsoever news may reach me in the course of the day, teach me to accept it with a calm soul and the firm conviction that all is subject to Your holy will.

Direct my thoughts and feelings in all my words and actions. In all unexpected occurrences, do not let me forget that all is sent down from You.

Grant that I may deal straightforwardly and wisely with every member of my family, neither embarrassing nor saddening anyone.

O Lord, grant me the strength to endure the fatigue of the coming day and all the events that take place during it. Direct my will and teach me to pray, to believe, to hope, to be patient, to forgive, and to love. Amen.

Through the prayers of our Holy Fathers, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy upon us and save us. Amen.

Evening Prayers

Before Sleep — enter the presence of God, ready to engage in unseen warfare, and make the Sign of the Cross, saying:

Usual Beginning

In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

Glory to You, our God, glory to You.

O Heavenly King, Comforter, Spirit of Truth, who are everywhere present and fill all things, Treasury of good gifts and Giver of Life, come and abide in us, and cleanse us of all impurity, and save our souls, O Good One.

Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us. (3 times)

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Most Holy Trinity, have mercy on us. O Lord, wash away our sins, O Master, pardon our transgressions. O Holy One, visit and heal our infirmities, for Your Name’s sake.

Lord, have mercy. (3 times)

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your Name. Your Kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.

Prayer of St. Macarius the Great

O Eternal God and King of all creation, who have granted me to arrive at this hour, forgive the sins I have committed this day in deed, word and thought. Cleanse, O Lord, my humble soul from every defilement of flesh and spirit.

Grant that I may pass through this night in peace. When I rise from my humble bed, may I please Your most Holy Name all the days of my life and trample under foot the enemies that wage war against me, whether bodily or spiritual.

Deliver me, Lord, from vain thoughts that defile me and from evil lusts. For Yours is the Kingdom, and the power, and the glory: of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Prayer to Christ

And now, as I lay down to sleep, O Master, grant me repose both of body and soul, and keep me from the dark sleep of sin and from the sensuous pleasure of the dark passions of the night.

Still the assaults of passion and quench the fiery darts of the wicked one which are thrown cunningly at me. Calm the commotions of my flesh and put away all earthly and material thoughts as I sleep.

Grant to me, O God, a watchful mind, chaste thoughts, a sober heart, and a gentle sleep, free from all the fantasies of Satan.

And raise me up again at the hour of prayer, established in Your commandments and holding steadfast within, the remembrance of Your judgments.

Give me the words of Your glorification all night long, that I may praise, bless, and glorify Your most honourable and magnificent Name: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Prayer to Your Guardian Angel

Holy angel who watches out for my wretched soul, forsake me not, and depart not from me because of my lack of restraint and wickedness. Prevent Satan from ruling with his might, this mortal body of mine, and hold me by my feeble hand and lead me in the path of salvation.

O holy Guardian Angel of God, protector of my wretched soul and body, forgive me for all the ways I have saddened you all the days of my life. And though I have sinned today, be my shelter this night. Protect me that I may not upset God with any sin. Intercede with the Lord for me, that He may confirm me in His fear, and reveal me a worthy servant of His goodness. Amen.

Enlighten my eyes, Christ God, that I fall not asleep to death and that my enemy may not say of me: I have overcome him.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.

Be the Helper of my soul, O God, for I walk among many snares. Deliver me from them, O Good One, and save me, for You love mankind.

Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Let us ever hymn the most glorious Mother of God, who is holier than the holy Angels. With heart and lips let us confess that she is the Theotokos, for she truly bore God incarnate for us and prays ceaselessly for our souls. Amen.

Through the prayers of our Holy Fathers, Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy upon us and save us. Amen.

Common Prayers

Prayers said often, alone or with others — found in nearly every Greek Orthodox prayer book.

The Jesus Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.

Prayer of St. Ephraim the Syrian

Said especially during Great Lent, with prostrations

O Lord and Master of my life, take from me the spirit of sloth, despair, lust of power, and idle talk. (prostration)

But give rather the spirit of chastity, humility, patience, and love to Your servant. (prostration)

Yes, O Lord and King, grant me to see my own transgressions, and not to judge my brother, for blessed are You to the ages of ages. Amen. (prostration)

It Is Truly Meet

Hymn to the Theotokos (Axion Estin)

It is truly meet to bless you, O Theotokos, ever-blessed and most blameless, and the Mother of our God. More honorable than the Cherubim, and more glorious beyond compare than the Seraphim, without corruption you gave birth to God the Word: true Theotokos, we magnify you.

Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos

The Angelic Salutation

Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos, Mary full of grace, the Lord is with you. Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb, for you have borne the Savior of our souls.

Prayer of Confession

A daily confession of sins

O Lord my God, I know that I am not worthy nor sufficient that You should come under the roof of the house of my soul, for it is entirely desolate and in ruins, and You have not with me a fitting place to lay Your head.

But as from the highest heaven You humbled Yourself for our sake, so now conform Yourself to my lowliness. And as You consented to lie in a cave and a manger of dumb beasts, so consent also to enter the manger of my dumb soul and my defiled body.

I confess to You, O Lord, the sins I have committed this day, and in all the days of my life, in thought, word, and deed; through all my senses, seen and unseen, known and unknown. Forgive me, O Master, for I have sinned against You more than any man, and have been the cause of my own soul's affliction and defilement.

Cleanse me, O Lord, and grant me a contrite heart, that I may return to You with my whole soul, and sin no more against You, my God and my Savior. Amen.

Grace Before Meals

Christ our God, bless the food and drink of Your servants, for You are holy, always, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

Before the Meal — A Prayer of Gratitude

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, we give thanks to you for this day and this meal.

For this food, for the hands that grew it, prepared it, and set it before us — thank you.

We don’t take this table for granted. Too many sit down to less today.

Let this meal strengthen our bodies, and let this gathering strengthen what’s between us — patience, honesty, good humor.

We entrust this hour to your goodness, and to each other. Amen.

Before the Meal — A Family Gathering Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, we give You thanks for calling us together around this table.

Thank You for the ones seated here, and for the ones who could not be — for every hand that had a part in this meal, and for the years that brought this family to this day.

Forgive us, Lord, for the harsh word held onto too long, the distance we let grow, the love we assumed but did not show.

Bless this food to our bodies and this gathering to our hearts. Where there is division among us, bring peace; where there is silence, bring warmth; where there is distance, bring nearness again.

We entrust this table, and each one seated at it, into Your keeping.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

Grace After Meals

We thank You, O Christ our God, that You have filled us with Your earthly gifts; deprive us not of Your heavenly Kingdom, but as You came among Your disciples, O Savior, and gave them peace, come also to us and save us. Amen.

Prayer Before Study

O Lord Jesus Christ, our God, who is the Wisdom of the Father and the fountain of all understanding, send down upon me now the grace of Your Holy Spirit.

Open the eyes of my mind to receive what I read, and open my heart to be changed by it, not puffed up by it. Let me seek You, not merely knowledge about You. Guard me from vanity of mind and from a spirit of argument without love. Grant me patience with what I do not yet understand, and humility before what I do.

As the Prophet David sang, “The unfolding of Your words gives light; it gives understanding to the simple” (Psalm 118:130, LXX). So enlighten now my understanding, that what I learn may bear fruit in a right mind, a humble heart, and a life pleasing to You.

Through the prayers of the Holy Fathers, and all the Saints, have mercy on me and save me. Amen.

A Psalm for Protection

Prayed at Small Compline and traditionally used for protection from fear, affliction, and the “noonday demon.”

Psalm of Protection

Psalm 90

He who dwells in the help of the Most High shall abide in the shelter of the God of heaven.

He shall say to the Lord, You are my helper and my refuge, my God; I will hope in Him.

For He shall deliver you from the snare of the hunters, and from every troubling word.

He shall overshadow you with His shoulders, and under His wings you shall have hope; His truth shall cover you with a shield.

You shall not be afraid of terror by night, nor of the arrow that flies by day,

Nor of the thing that walks in darkness, nor of the calamity and the demon of noonday.

A thousand shall fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand; but it shall not come near you.

You shall only consider this with your eyes, and you shall see the reward of sinners.

For You, O Lord, are my hope; you have made the Most High your refuge.

No evil shall come near you, nor shall a scourge come near your dwelling.

For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways.

On their hands they shall bear you up, lest you dash your foot against a stone.

You shall tread upon the asp and the basilisk, and you shall trample the lion and the dragon underfoot.

Because he has set his hope on Me, I will deliver him; I will shelter him because he has known My name.

He shall cry to Me, and I will listen to him; I am with him in affliction, and I will rescue him and glorify him.

With length of days I will satisfy him, and I will show him My salvation.

For Healing

Said for one who is sick or suffering.

Prayer for Healing

O Lord Jesus Christ, our God, Physician of our souls and bodies, look down in Your mercy upon Your servant [name], who lies in the weakness of sickness.

You who healed the sick by a word, who raised up the paralytic, and who wept at the tomb of Your friend Lazarus — stretch forth Your unseen hand and grant [him/her] healing, if it be according to Your holy will.

Grant [him/her] patience in suffering, peace in [his/her] soul, and strength in [his/her] body. Let no illness separate [him/her] from Your love. Whether You raise [him/her] up in health or call [him/her] nearer to Yourself through this trial, let Your will, not mine, be done.

Through the prayers of the Theotokos, of the Unmercenary Physicians Cosmas and Damian, of the Great Martyr and Healer Panteleimon, and of all the saints, have mercy on Your servant [name], and on us all. Amen.

For the Departed

Said in memory of loved ones who have fallen asleep in the Lord.

Prayer for the Departed

O God of spirits and of all flesh, who has trampled down death and overthrown the devil, and given life to Your world, do You, the same Lord, give rest to the souls of Your departed servants in a place of brightness, a place of refreshment, a place of repose, where all sickness, sighing, and sorrow have fled away.

Pardon every transgression which they have committed, whether by word, or deed, or thought. For You are a good God and love mankind; because there is no man who lives and sins not, for You only are without sin, Your righteousness is to all eternity, and Your word is truth.

For You are the Resurrection, the Life, and the Repose of Your departed servants, O Christ our God, and to You we ascribe glory, together with Your Unoriginate Father, and Your All-Holy, Good, and Life-Giving Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

May their memory be eternal.

Prayers for Holy Communion

Said quietly while approaching the chalice, and again after receiving.

Pre-Communion Prayer

St. John Chrysostom

I believe, O Lord, and I confess that You are truly the Christ, the Son of the Living God, who came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief.

I believe also that this is truly Your own pure Body, and that this is truly Your own precious Blood. Therefore, I pray to You: have mercy on me, and forgive my transgressions, both voluntary and involuntary, of word and of deed, of knowledge and of ignorance.

And make me worthy without condemnation to partake of Your own pure Mysteries, for the remission of sins and unto life everlasting. Amen.

We Have Seen the True Light

Hymn sung after receiving Holy Communion

We have seen the true Light; we have received the heavenly Spirit; we have found the true Faith, worshiping the undivided Trinity, for the Trinity has saved us.

Post-Communion Prayer

O Lord Jesus Christ our God, let Your sacred Body be to me for eternal life, and Your precious Blood for forgiveness of sins. Let this Eucharist be to me for joy, health, and gladness. And in Your dread Second Coming, make me, a sinner, worthy to stand at the right hand of Your glory, through the intercessions of Your most pure Mother and of all Your Saints. Amen.

Family Prayers

For Father and Brothers

O Lord Jesus Christ our God, who taught us to honor father and mother and to love our brethren as ourselves, look down in Your mercy upon my father and my brothers. Preserve them in health of soul and body; guard them from every evil, temptation, and sorrow; and guide their steps in the way of Your commandments.

Grant them peace in their homes, wisdom in their labors, and a firm hope in Your providence. Draw them ever nearer to Yourself, that together we may stand before You in the unity of faith and the bond of love, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

For My Wife, Stephanie

O Lord our God, who joined man and wife together and blessed their union as a great mystery, I entrust to Your loving care my wife, Stephanie. Grant her health, strength, and peace of soul; fill her heart with Your grace and comfort her in every trial.

Bless our life together, that we may grow in love for one another and in love for You, bearing one another’s burdens and rejoicing in one another’s joys. Keep her under the shelter of Your wings, O Christ our God, through the prayers of the Theotokos and all the saints. Amen.

For My Daughters and Nephew

Mariah, Victoria, Sophia, and Matthew

O Lord Jesus Christ our God, who blessed the little children and said, “Let the little children come to Me, for of such is the Kingdom of Heaven,” watch over Your servants Mariah, Victoria, Sophia, and Matthew with Your loving care. Guard their souls and bodies from every harm; guide their minds toward truth and their hearts toward virtue.

Grant them good health, a spirit of obedience, and love for You and for one another. Protect them from the snares of this world, and raise up in them a firm and unshaken faith.

Surround them with Your holy angels, and through the prayers of the Great Martyr Marina, the Great Martyr Victoria, Saint Sophia the Martyr, and the Holy Apostle Matthew, lead them in the path of salvation, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

An Orthodox Prayer for the Protection of My Family

Pray as an ongoing guard over your household, asking that harm be kept away before it arrives

O Lord Jesus Christ, our God, who Yourself grew in wisdom and stature, and who commanded us to look after one another in love: look down with Your boundless mercy upon my wife, my children, and every member of my family.

O Master, You know the dangers of this world and the heavy spiritual warfare that surrounds us all today. I confess my worries to You, and I lay the safety and wellbeing of my whole family into Your hands. Send down Your Holy Spirit to enlighten our minds and steady our hearts. Grant us the wisdom to discern right from wrong, the strength to resist temptation, and the courage to flee from harmful influences, toxic relationships, and the deceitful traps of this age.

Command Your Holy Angels to camp around each one of us. Protect us, O Lord, from physical harm, sudden accidents, sickness, and spiritual blindness. Keep us safe in our comings and goings, whether under one roof or scattered out in the world.

Guide our footsteps as we walk into whatever lies ahead. Be our ultimate Guardian, Provider, and Guide, covering each member of my family with the prayers we may otherwise lack. Grant us clean hearts, sharp minds, and a deep reverence for Your truth. May we grow to be steadfast, honorable, and full of faith, honoring one another and glorifying Your holy name.

Through the intercessions of the Holy Theotokos, the holy Guardian Angels, and all the Saints who have pleased You from the beginning of time.

For Yours is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

A Prayer for Deliverance from the Unseen Enemy, for Myself and My Household

Pray when you sense a dark pressure already at work against your home, rather than simply asking for it to be kept away

Lord Jesus Christ, our God, You alone know how the enemy works against Your people, even when we cannot see it. Look on me, on my wife, Stephanie, and on my children, Mariah, Victoria, Sophia, and Matthew, and deliver us from every attack of the evil one — seen and unseen — that is pressing on our home.

You know what is troubling us, Lord, even though it hides itself from us. You see what is wearing down our bodies, unsettling our minds, and turning peace into discord. I am not asking You to explain it. I am asking You to break it.

By the power of Your Cross, drive off whatever dark power has attached itself to this family. The way You delivered the man among the tombs, the way You calmed the storm, the way You spoke to the fever and it left — speak over my house now, and let this leave too.

Watch over my wife, Stephanie. Strengthen her where she is worn thin, restore what has been drained from her, and do not let any shadow rest on her heart.

Watch over my children. Do not let fear, confusion, or darkness take root in them — let them grow up covered by Your hand, knowing only Your peace.

And watch over me, Lord, so I can be a covering for this family instead of the door the harm gets in through. Wherever I have opened a door to the enemy, knowingly or not, close it, and forgive me.

Send Your angel to stand guard around us. Let the prayers of the Theotokos and all the saints surround us like a wall. Let nothing that is not from You find a place to rest in this home.

We are asking not because we understand what is troubling us, but because we know You, and nothing hidden is hidden from You. Have mercy on us, and deliver us — for You are our God, and to You we give glory, together with Your Father from everlasting, and Your all-holy, good, and life-giving Spirit, now and always, and to the ages of ages. Amen.

A Parent’s Prayer for an Adult Daughter and Her Husband

O Lord Jesus Christ, our God, who blessed the wedding in Cana of Galilee and declared marriage to be honorable, look down with love and mercy upon my daughter and her husband.

Master, You have entrusted her to my care since the days of her childhood, and I thank You for the joy of seeing her grow into maturity. Forgive me for the times my heart clings to her as a child, and grant me the wisdom and humility to step back, respecting the independent life and home she has established. Help me to love her with a supportive spirit, honoring her adulthood and placing her completely into Your all-powerful hands.

Bless their union, O Lord. Preserve them in peace, concord, and a mature love that grows deeper with each passing year. Grant them length of days, fair children, progress in life and faith, and fill their house with Your good things. Crown their marriage with Your grace, that they may be of one heart and one mind, walking together in the path of Your commandments.

Be their shield and protector, O Holy Trinity. Guide them through the heavy choices and quiet battles of adult life. Distance them from all harmful influences, and grant them a spirit of wisdom, patience, and mutual forgiveness. When trials arise, steady their hearts with Your supernatural peace and draw them ever closer to You.

Through the prayers of the Holy Theotokos and of all the Saints, bless this new generation, that they may live to Your glory and find salvation in Your heavenly kingdom.

For Yours is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
